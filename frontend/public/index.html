JabWeMeet Safety Pledge

Our commitment to creating safe, dignified, offline experiences.

1. Strict Identity Screening: Every attendee registers with mobile verification and identity checks. We reserve the right to deny admission to unverified profiles.


2. Safe Public Venues: All events take place in vetted public locations — partner cafes, boutique hotel lounges, private studios with attentive staff.


3. On-Ground Event Hosts: Every JabWeMeet experience is moderated by trained hosts who oversee dynamics, introduce games, and ensure personal boundaries are respected.


4. Consent-First Culture: Sharing phone numbers or personal handles is 100% voluntary. You decide if and when you want to connect outside the event.


5. Zero Tolerance: Any harassment, inappropriate behavior, or boundary violation leads to immediate expulsion and lifetime banning.
